# Reference PDP template (example — replace with ours)

Rules for every product page:
- Tone: confident, plain English, second person. No exclamation marks. No medical claims.
- Above the fold: product name, one-line promise, star rating line, price with compare-at, 3 benefit bullets, "Add to cart" CTA.
- Images: 1 hero on white, 2 lifestyle, 1 benefits infographic, 1 size/what's-in-the-box.

## 1. Hero
Headline (max 8 words) + subhead (max 20 words) + 3 bullets + CTA.

## 2. The problem
2–3 short paragraphs on the pain the product solves, in the customer's words.

## 3. How it works
3 numbered steps, each with an image.

## 4. Benefits grid
4–6 tiles: icon idea + 3–6 word title + one sentence.

## 5. Comparison table
Us vs "traditional option" on 5 rows.

## 6. Reviews
6 reviews, 4–5 stars, first name + initial, one sentence each.

## 7. Guarantee + shipping
30-day money-back, ships in 24h, tracked delivery.

## 8. FAQ
5–7 questions.

## 9. Final CTA
Restate the promise + price anchor + CTA.
